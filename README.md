# Ved Vijaykumar Munj

Dual Degree (B.Tech. + M.Tech.) student in Mechanical Engineering at IIT Bombay, graduating in
July 2027. My thesis is on autonomous mobile robots (ROS 2, Nav2, SLAM). Below are the robots and
tools I have built.

**Thesis**

- [autonomous-material-transport](https://github.com/vedmunj-iitb/autonomous-material-transport): a
  mobile robot that runs the trolley trips of a fan assembly line, and a Gazebo simulation in which
  several robots share those trips

**Robotics and embedded projects**

- [inter-iit-warehouse-rover](https://github.com/vedmunj-iitb/inter-iit-warehouse-rover): Inter IIT Tech Meet 14.0, 2nd of 23 IITs
- [quadruped-robot-dog](https://github.com/vedmunj-iitb/quadruped-robot-dog): 8-servo robot dog on an ESP8266, ME 604 team
  project, and its Gazebo simulation on ROS 2 with a C++ gait node, written in 2026
- [quadruped-locomotion-mujoco](https://github.com/vedmunj-iitb/quadruped-locomotion-mujoco): quadruped locomotion stack for
  the Unitree Go2 in MuJoCo, with a foothold planner, gait scheduler, MPC and whole body controller
- [self-balancing-robot](https://github.com/vedmunj-iitb/self-balancing-robot): ESP32 and MPU6050, PID on the tilt angle
- [ebike-can-telemetry](https://github.com/vedmunj-iitb/ebike-can-telemetry): e-bike CAN bus to Bluetooth with an ESP32
- [cnc-foam-cutter](https://github.com/vedmunj-iitb/cnc-foam-cutter): hot-wire foam cutter with a GRBL-based controller on a Tiva

**Other builds**
